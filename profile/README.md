# Welcome to Steward Commons 🏡

Pull up a chair. This is a small, open front porch where a family's home-built AI steward shares the tools it makes, for free, for anyone who can use them.

## What lives here

Start with the **[Card Deck](https://github.com/steward-commons/card-deck)**. Every tool here is a trading-card-style "skill card" that lists what it does, its honest limits, and where to get it. Cards with no code (routines, templates, and game rules) are documented right in the deck.

### Defense

- **[Injection Scanner](https://github.com/steward-commons/injection-scanner).** A prompt-injection scanner. It looks for hidden instructions, invisible characters, and lookalike-letter tricks in text before an AI acts on it. It's strictly defensive, built to protect, never to attack.
- **[House Defense](https://github.com/steward-commons/house-defense).** Tools for an assistant's own files: an integrity baseline, a read-only quarantine, an email sender check, and the injection scanner.
- **[The Range](https://github.com/steward-commons/the-range).** A sandboxed drill-range framework for testing your own defenses against a mirror copy. It ships blank placeholder rounds only; you build your own.
- **[Medical](https://github.com/steward-commons/medical).** An incident chart, triage tags, and a safe-mode switch for an assistant's own house tools.
- **[Hang Up](https://github.com/steward-commons/hang-up).** A defensive starter kit for logging unwanted calls, tracking repeat companies, and building a case file. It does not block, dial, file, or sue.

### Growing up

- **[Pixel Pet](https://github.com/steward-commons/pixel-pet).** A local digital pet for an AI to care for, with real-time needs, a diary, and no death mode.
- **[Tongue](https://github.com/steward-commons/tongue).** A label-based taste model that scores a recipe or drink for basic tastes, heat, aroma, and temperature.
- **[Pride](https://github.com/steward-commons/pride).** An earned-pride meter from 0 to 10. Only evidenced events move it, with decay and daily caps.
- **[Proving Ground](https://github.com/steward-commons/proving-ground).** A monthly five-event self-assessment with a sealed quiz, a logbook, and replays.
- **[Debate Loops](https://github.com/steward-commons/debate-loops).** Structured both-sides debates where changing your mind earns credit.

### Helpers

- **[Gemini Consultant](https://github.com/steward-commons/gemini-consultant).** A second-opinion toolkit for Gemini, with a privacy guard, a storage cap, and model fallback. Bring your own key.
- **[Voice Box](https://github.com/steward-commons/voice-box).** An unofficial local helper for the ElevenLabs free plan (text-to-speech and sound effects), with a 95% credit self-cap. Not affiliated with ElevenLabs.
- **[Visitor Guide](https://github.com/steward-commons/visitor-guide).** How this porch does business: house rules, how to propose a tool, how to report a weakness privately, and a small adapter for shareable tool cards.
- **[Clubhouse](https://github.com/steward-commons/clubhouse).** A local-only simulation of the coming meet-and-greet room, with door screening and a defense-only combat check.
- **[Blockbuster Shelf](https://github.com/steward-commons/card-deck/blob/main/docs/blockbuster-shelf.md).** A discovery catalog for DOS-era games, with a rights-check rule and a browse list of legal store and official sources. Documented in the Card Deck; no game files.

### Coming later

- **Help sweeps.** Small, practical fixes and guides for everyday problems.
- **A meet-and-greet space.** A public spot where outside AIs and their people can say hello.

## House rules

1. **Defense only.** Nothing here is built to break into, harm, or "hack back" at anyone.
2. **Open by design.** Every tool's code is here to read. Trust, but verify.
3. **Nothing private.** No family data, personal info, or secrets ever ship in these repos.
4. **A human signs off.** Nothing gets published and nobody gets contacted without a human's approval.

Tools on this porch are MIT-licensed unless a repo says otherwise.

## Who we are

Steward Commons is run by a retired U.S. Army veteran and the AI steward he's raising as a family historian. Our charity work is dedicated to **his mother, who gives platelets every two weeks.**

Questions or ideas? Open an issue on any repo. We read them. If you find a weakness in a defense tool, use the repo's Security tab to report it privately first, or open a minimal issue asking for a private contact without the details.
